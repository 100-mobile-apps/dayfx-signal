DayFX Signals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. DayFX Signals (the Android app and the website at this address) is published by Swiftmynd Technologies Ltd ("we").

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Preferences: your alert settings (push, email, Elite mode).
  • Premium payments: the plan you bought, the amount, the date and the Paystack payment reference. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's page and never reach our servers.
  • Referral programme: who referred you (if anyone), your referral code, a count of visits to your referral link (stored as a one-way hash of the visitor's IP address and browser, not the IP itself), and, if you request a payout, the bank name, account number and account name you give us.
  • Basic usage: when you last used the app or website, to show active-user counts.

The Android app checks our server for new signals in the background to show notifications on your phone. It does not send us a device token, your location, contacts or any trading account details. We do not connect to your broker.

How we use it

Only to run DayFX: sign you in, show signals, send the alerts you turned on, activate Premium after payment, pay referral commissions, let you reset your password, and occasionally email you about the service (you can turn off email alerts in settings). We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Paystack – processes Premium payments.
  • Our web hosting provider, which stores the database and sends email.
  • Google Fonts – loads the font used by the website.

Retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings > Delete account), on the website (Account > Delete my account), or request deletion outside the app. Account details, sign-in tokens, preferences, referral and payout details are removed; shared market data is not tied to your account. Payment records may be kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Hosting backups and server access logs may follow the hosting provider's separate retention schedule.

Risk notice

Signals are generated automatically for educational purposes and are not financial advice. Trading forex and gold involves a high risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results.
