DayFX Signals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. DayFX Signals is published by Swiftmynd Technologies Ltd ("we").

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Notification data: notification preferences. Current mobile alerts use locally scheduled API polling, not Firebase device tokens.
  • Preferences: your alert settings (push, email, Elite mode).

We do not collect your location, contacts, trading account details or payment information. We do not connect to your broker.

How we use it

Only to run the app: sign you in, send the alerts you have enabled, and let you reset your password. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Our hosting provider, which stores the app database.

Retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings > Delete account), or request deletion outside the app. Account details, sign-in tokens, preferences and any linked device tokens are removed; shared market data is not tied to your account. Hosting backups and server access logs may follow the hosting provider's separate retention schedule.

Risk notice

Signals are generated automatically for educational purposes and are not financial advice. Trading forex and gold involves a high risk of loss. Past results do not guarantee future results.
