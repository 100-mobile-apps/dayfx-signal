Delete your DayFX Signals account

DayFX Signals is published by Swiftmynd Technologies Ltd. You can request deletion even if you cannot open or sign in to the app.

Delete in the app

If you can sign in, open Settings > Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account immediately.

Request deletion outside the app

Contact us on WhatsApp and include the email address used for your DayFX Signals account. We will verify account ownership before processing your request. Do not send your password or reset codes.

Request account deletion on WhatsApp

If the button does not open WhatsApp, message +234 701 140 1387 with your deletion request and account email.

Data affected

Account name, email, password hash, sign-in tokens, account notification preferences and any account-linked device tokens are deleted. The app clears cached account details when it next checks the deleted session online. Shared market signals, news and aggregated results are not associated with individual accounts and are not deleted. Hosting backups and server access logs may follow the hosting provider's separate retention schedule.

See our privacy policy for more information.
